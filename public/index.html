    My PC

    Checking...
    1. Press Download PC helper (do this on your Windows PC).
    2. Open the downloaded file start-ghost-pc.bat. If Windows warns, click More info, then Run anyway.
    3. A small Ghost window opens. Say "Hey Ghost", wait for "I'm here", then say what you want. Keep the window open (it can start with Windows if you tick the box).

    Open Ghost from your main address when you download, so the helper knows where to connect. Ghost asks you to tap Confirm (or press Yes in a box on the PC if you used your voice) before it shuts down, restarts, sleeps, closes an app or types. Your voice is understood on the PC itself; nothing is sent until you say Hey Ghost.
